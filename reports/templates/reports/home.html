What can you report?

SafeAlert can be used to report a range of community safety concerns.

Road Safety

Report hazards and concerns affecting roads and drivers.

Street Lighting

Report damaged or inadequate street lighting.

Crime & Security

Report community concerns relating to security and public safety.

Environmental

Report environmental hazards affecting the local community.

Pedestrian Safety

Report hazards affecting pedestrians and public spaces.

School Safety

Report safety concerns around schools and educational areas.

Property Hazards

Report hazards involving buildings, structures or property.

Other

Report other community safety concerns that do not fit another category.

Help improve your community

Reporting a safety concern helps create a clearer picture of issues affecting the community.
