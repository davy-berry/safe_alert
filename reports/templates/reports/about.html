About SafeAlert

Building safer communities together

SafeAlert helps communities report local safety issues, identify risks and track problems through to resolution.

What is SafeAlert?

SafeAlert is a community safety intelligence platform designed to make it easier for people to report local safety concerns and for administrators to manage and prioritise those reports.

Instead of individual reports becoming isolated pieces of information, SafeAlert brings them together so that safety issues can be reviewed, prioritised and tracked.

The platform provides a structured process from the initial report through to action and resolution.

Our purpose

Local safety problems can affect people every day. Issues such as poor street lighting, road hazards, environmental problems or concerns around schools can easily go unnoticed or remain unresolved.

SafeAlert provides a central place where these concerns can be reported and managed, helping turn community observations into useful safety information.

SafeAlert focuses on

  • Community reporting
  • Risk identification
  • Report prioritisation
  • Issue tracking
  • Community safety intelligence

How SafeAlert works

A structured process for managing community safety issues.

Report

Community members can report local safety issues and provide relevant information about the problem and its location.

Analyse

Reports can be assessed using information such as their category and calculated risk score.

Prioritise

Risk scores help categorise reports by priority so that higher-risk issues can be identified.

Action

Administrators can manage reports, update their status and track progress towards resolution.

A community-focused approach

SafeAlert is designed around the idea that local communities are an important source of information about the safety issues affecting their area.

By giving people a simple way to report concerns and providing administrators with tools to review and manage those reports, SafeAlert creates a clearer connection between reporting and action.
